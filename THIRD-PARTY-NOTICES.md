## Preview stylesheet provenance

`media/obsidian.css` is generated by `tools/build-preview-css.mjs` from
`styles/preview-theme.css`. That source file is the author's own Obsidian CSS
snippet (kept in the author's vault as `notation-purple.css`); it is original
work by Yifu Ding and is covered by this project's MIT license.

The snippet is an override layer written against the community Obsidian theme
**Notation 2** by Ha'ani Whitlock (<https://github.com/Bluemoondragon07/obsidian-notation-2>),
which is released under the Unlicense. Only the author's own override rules are
ported into the preview; the Notation 2 theme body is not copied. Notation 2 is
credited here as the visual reference. The Unlicense places no obligations on
this distribution.
